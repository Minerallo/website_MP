About me

I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at GFZ Potsdam (section 2.5, Geodynamic modeling), where I am investigating the effect of surface processes on plate tectonic activity over the last 1ba, I have developed an advanced coupled model between geodynamics, surface processes and climate code.

3D model computed with ASPECT. The model shows topography generated from self-consistent geodynamical processes. Cold subductions (blue) are sinking into the mantle whereas warm mantle plumes (red) are rising towards the surface.

I did my PhD between the University of Potsdam and the Helmholtz Centre Potsdam GFZ, Department 2.5 Geodynamic Modelling. There I developed regional scale numerical models of Chilean subduction to better understand the mechanism behind the evolution of the Andean surface and, more generally, deformation at convergent margins. In particular, my PhD thesis focused on the role of subduction dynamics and its interaction with the overriding plate. I am also interested in developing new tools to simulate the Earth's active tectonics. During my studies, I have created a code that calculates the coseismic contribution of earthquakes to the gravity field (the code is available upon request). Additionally, I have been involved in paleomagnetic studies, including measuring the Anisotropy of Magnetic Susceptibility to gain a deeper understanding of the mode of emplacement of plutons, as well as calculating paleomagnetic pole positions and discussing their implications for the closure age of the Mongol-Okhotsk ocean.

I have also participated in many field schools and works as decribed in my my curriculum .
