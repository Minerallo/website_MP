Numerical Models of Geodynamics | ASPECT + FastScape

Models below are built using the ASPECT code and visualized with ParaView.

Slab Buckling

A cold mantle is sinking in an adiabatic mantle. The jump of viscosity at 660 km depth causes the slab to buckle on itself. The video shows the compositional phases and strain rate.

Flat Subduction

The oceanic plate subducts with a low angle causing flat subduction. When the slab steepens back, the stress is accommodated by deformation of the overriding plate — a process potentially key in forming the Andes and Laramide.

Subduction & Surface Processes

Surface processes reshape convergent boundaries. Sediments eroded and transported to the trench lubricate the subduction interface, influencing stress and deformation in the orogen. This model shows the ASPECT–FastScape coupling.

Subduction Dynamics and Continent Deformation

Subduction dynamics control orogeny. This model links slab behavior (buckling, flattening) with upper-plate deformation, offering insight into the Andean orogeny and similar tectonic systems.

Data-Driven Subduction Model

Published present-day lithospheric structural model of the Southern Central Andes used to explore how slab geometry and inherited structures in the overriding plate influence deformation localization.

Global Mantle Convection on Earth

Calibrated global numerical models of plate tectonics and mantle dynamics. Visualized at the Berlin Planetarium, showing subduction (blue) and rising mantle plumes (red).
