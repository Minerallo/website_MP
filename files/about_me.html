About Me

I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at GFZ Potsdam (Section 2.5, Geodynamic Modelling), where my research focuses on understanding the development of different tectonic regimes, from Earth’s plate tectonics to Venus’ ridge-only and squishy-lid styles. I am developing an advanced geodynamic model to investigate these processes, with the long-term goal of coupling mantle dynamics and surface processes at the global scale.

I completed my PhD jointly at the University of Potsdam and the Helmholtz Centre Potsdam GFZ (Department 2.5, Geodynamic Modelling), where I developed regional-scale numerical models of Chilean subduction to better understand the mechanisms driving Andean surface evolution and deformation at convergent margins. My PhD research primarily focused on the role of subduction dynamics and its interaction with the overriding plate.

Beyond research, I serve as Co-Editor-in-Chief of the EGU Geodynamics Blog and co-lead Working Group 4 of the EUROBIG (Biogeodynamics) COST Action— an initiative dedicated to bridging geodynamics, biology, and climate sciences.

I have also contributed to the development of new computational tools to estimate the coseismic contribution of earthquakes to the gravity field, and I have participated in numerous field schools and collaborative international research efforts.
