Online Presence

Since 2021, I have been Co-Editor-in-Chief of the EGU Geodynamics Blog, after serving as a regular editor for several years. This platform receives worldwide attention, with over 60,000 readers each year. My role involves coordinating an international editorial team, promoting early-career researchers, and highlighting emerging topics in tectonics, mantle dynamics, and numerical modeling.

I am also involved in the COST Action CA23150 – EUROBIG (“Pan-European Biogeodynamics Network”), where I co-lead Working Package 4 (WP4) dedicated to outreach, education, and digital networking. Our aim is to connect geodynamics, climate, and biological sciences through collaborative, interdisciplinary initiatives.

SciANS is a blog where I share insights about geodynamics, publications, and science communication, bridging my interests in numerical modeling, visualization, and outreach. It serves as a personal space to communicate science in accessible ways, connect research communities, and discuss ideas across disciplines.

Knowlapps – Geolapps was my first educational website, created to share my passion for geology and science. It contains lectures, field experiences, and early research projects that reflect my formative years in Earth sciences.
