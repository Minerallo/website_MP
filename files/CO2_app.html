Key ideas

  • Storage is not magic. CO₂ needs suitable porous rock, depth, and a sealing caprock.
  • Monitoring matters. Surveys and sensors help check where CO₂ goes through time.
  • Trust matters. Safe storage needs transparency, clear rules, and long-term responsibility.
  • Use matters. Storing CO₂ underground is different from using CO₂ to produce more oil.
